# Contact-presence v8: restricted tethered result

This research branch tests an explicit engineering contact-presence encoder on the retained real connectome. It does not repair or qualify free walking, food seeking, fighting, or competitive strategy in the production Arena. Canonical neural topology, signs, LIF dynamics, the v7 compiled body fixture, and the original 15 behavior gates are unchanged.

## Executed panel and result

The frozen panel completed 96 scientific one-second conditions plus three exact repeats. It compares canonical WT, the pinned official Nectar artifact, and an existing submitted olfactory ×1.08 artifact. The submitted artifact's owner ID is provenance, not proof that the requesting human or an AI authored it. Subjects share the same imposed initial conditions and controls; realized contact can differ through feedback.

The engineering encoder injects 48 mV when actual probe contact on the corresponding leg is present. This amplitude is a declared engineered voltage-equivalent input, not calibrated physiological transduction. The thorax is tethered; only six knee outputs are tested. No sensor/world/reward signal bypasses the neural network into the motor decoder.

Five of twelve local subject/context/leg conditions pass the frozen causal/body thresholds:

- Neutral odor, RM: WT, official, and submitted.
- Odor OFF, WT: LF and RM.

Neutral LF remains below the local body threshold. RF never qualifies. Official/submitted OFF and direct LM/LH/RH probes were not run. States 42/43 are deterministic posture perturbations, not biological replications.

Only WT-minus-official neutral RM jointly resolves the paired neural and body thresholds in both states. The signed mean body differences are 0.0010679704459873908 and 0.001070626787827083 rad; the signed neural difference is 4.406266828146471 Hz in both states. Other pair/leg comparisons comprise three neural-only, four state-dependent, and one below-threshold result. These are engineering reporting thresholds, not population significance or evidence of competitive superiority.

## Verification and presentation

All 99 saved physical .20–.21 s continuations reproduce exactly. Three fixed subject neural .20–.50 s intervals are bitwise reproducible; an independent NumPy integrator also reproduced those three intervals without importing the Arena integrator/compiler. All three full repeats are exact. Verification binds 3,177 registered files, 1,388 original delivery entries, 420,520 raw contact rows, and 396 full neural checkpoints.

The final r3 display preserves the original payload and shows input as 100 actual held intervals. For the documented WT RF state-42 neutral example, drive starts at 0.21 s and the first differential afferent-spike endpoint is 0.22 s. The phenotype table explicitly remains neutral-only when selecting an OFF plot. See [presentation correction](CONTACT_PRESENTATION_V8_R3.md) for generation and resource-test contracts.

Final bounded correction reviews returned architecture **approve**, quality **comment**, tests **comment**, with no reject. Under the selected review rule this is done with advisory surfaced. The known advisory is a redundant identity-cue defect: separate held-current SVG subpaths restart the official dash pattern, so its short intervals appear solid even though the legend says dashed. Colors and numeric identities remain, but coincident current traces can be harder to distinguish. This does not alter timing, payload, or measurements and is not claimed fixed.

With the authoritative archive, 9 contact and 4 architecture tests pass. A fresh source-only checkout passes 8 contact and 4 architecture tests, with one explicit resource skip for the absent compiled fixture. Empty, partial, corrupt, broken-symlink, and regular-file fixture paths fail instead of skipping. Generated JavaScript/SVG was exercised over 576 selector combinations and 115,200 held intervals. This is minimal-DOM testing, not browser visual QA.
